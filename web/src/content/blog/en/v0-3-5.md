0.3.5 is the first release available on the project website. Download it from the [Install](/en/install/) page, where each file's SHA-256 checksum is listed at the bottom.

## Features

- **Four platforms**: macOS (Apple silicon), Linux, Windows and Steam Deck. Windows and Steam Deck support is still experimental.
- **Three languages**: full English and Chinese translations, alongside the original Japanese. Switch at any time in game.
- **HD art pack**: redrawn portraits, intermission backgrounds, world map, tactical maps and unit art, available as a separate download. You can switch back to the original graphics at any time.
- **Widescreen**: the picture adapts to fill screens with aspect ratios from 4:3 to 16:9.
- **Saves**: extra slots, autosaves and save exchange with ares, Project64, mupen64plus and RetroArch.
- **Reading and controls**: dialogue history, auto-reading, fast-forward, skip, interruptible battle animations and remapping.

## Fixes in this version

- The cursor on the pre-battle screen now moves by row, with a single button to switch between evade and defend.
- In Library, left and right move between series.
- Interface text now scales down to a minimum of 60% to keep it readable.

## Installation notes

- The macOS app is not notarized. On first launch, allow it under Privacy &amp; Security.
- The HD pack must match the app version. Update both together.
