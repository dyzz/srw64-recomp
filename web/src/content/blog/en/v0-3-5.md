0.3.5 is the first version on the website. Downloads are on the [Install](/en/install/) page, with every file’s SHA-256 at the bottom.

## What is in it

- **Four platforms**: macOS (Apple silicon), Linux, Windows and Steam Deck. Windows and Steam Deck are still experimental.
- **Three languages**: a full English and Chinese translation, and the original Japanese; <kbd>F7</kbd> switches at any time.
- **HD art pack**: redrawn portraits, intermission backgrounds, world map, tactical maps and unit art, as a separate download; <kbd>F6</kbd> switches back to the original at any time.
- **Widescreen**: the picture fills the screen anywhere from 4:3 to 16:9.
- **Saves**: extra slots, autosaves, and save exchange with ares, Project64, mupen64plus and RetroArch.
- **Reading and controls**: dialogue history, auto-reading, fast-forward and skip, ending battle animations early, rebinding.

## Fixes in this version

- The pre-battle screen moves the cursor by rows, and one button switches between evade and defend.
- Library: left and right jump between series.
- Interface text that shrinks to fit keeps a 60% floor.

## Known issues

- The macOS app is not notarized; allow it under Privacy &amp; Security on first launch.
- The HD pack only works with the app of the same version, so update both together.
