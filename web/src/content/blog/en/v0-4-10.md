0.4.10 is mostly fixes for what players reported after 0.4.9, with a few conveniences added. The HD pack has not changed and is still HD 1.1. Thanks to everyone who sent reports and recordings.

## Fixes

- **Windows: minimising the window with the settings or the pre-battle page open made the game stop responding.**
- **Loading the suspend save from the pre-battle page left the page over the map, holding every button.**
- **A wrong ROM closed the game without a word, like a crash.** Every platform now says what is wrong: an archive to extract first, the wrong size, another or a patched game, a missing file. On Android you can choose again; a Steam Deck in Game Mode says where to put rom.z64.
- **A line at the 4:3 edges of widescreen battle skies.** The fix is in the game, so the HD pack needs no update.
- With 4:3 kept in a wider window, the title's load menu and other pages sat off to the right.
- Unified weapon upgrades: at a machine swap in a stage opening (フルアーマー百式改 and others), the new machine's extra weapons started at level 0. They now take the old machine's level.
- A five-stat upgrade that reached the EW swap dropped the rest of the plan. It now goes on with the new machine, and the unit list says which one it became.

## New features

- **Return to the title screen**: a new entry in the map's menu and the intermission menu, also on F10, the stage info page and the desktop menu bar; it asks first. The original needed Z+START for this.
- **Portable mode (Windows and Linux)**: create an empty folder named user-data beside Marchwind64.exe (srw64 on Linux) and saves and settings live there, so the whole game folder, ROM included, can move to a USB stick or another PC. The first launch copies in your existing saves and settings; a normal exit syncs them back to the system folder, asking first when the saves differ and backing up the old ones.
- **The first launch follows the system language**: Simplified Chinese on a Chinese system, Japanese on a Japanese one, English otherwise; from Steam, Steam's language.
- **Desktop menu bar**: new Language, Tools and Help menus to switch the language, take a screenshot, toggle 2× speed, open the stage info, load the suspend save or export logs, each entry showing its key.
- **Three new cheats** (Options → Cheats): real damage, counted past 65535; EXP and money ×2/×5/×10; and an ally morale cap of 150, 200, 300 or 999.
- **Pilot pages show kills** (not for enemies).
- **Chapter title cards in brush lettering**: Japanese in a font traced from the original cards and laid out like them; Chinese and English in Ma Shan Zheng.

![Chapter title cards: the brush lettering in English, Chinese and Japanese](/media/title-cards-en.webp)

For problems, Options → Report or Tools → Export Logs in the menu bar exports a problem report. Which Android chips have been tested is on the [Android compatibility list](/en/android/).
