HD pack 1.2 comes out with [0.5.0](/en/blog/v0-5-0/). The main change is the unit icons on the map; the other changes are few, but all of them are things players see.

![Map unit icons: the original and HD 1.2](/media/hd12-icons.webp)

*The same map, the original on the left and HD 1.2 on the right*

## Map unit icons

The original map icons are 16×16 pixels, each a close-up of the unit's head (a ship is the whole ship seen from above). HD 1.1 upscaled them to 64×64 with upscaling models, keeping to the original's colours. That kept the pixel-art look, but 16 pixels hold very little, and many units were still hard to tell apart.

1.2 redraws them instead:

- **One at a time**: for each unit, the original icon sets the framing and the direction it faces, and the HD unit pose sets the colours and details; an image model draws it as 128×128 pixel art. All 320 icons were redrawn and checked one by one, and the ones with problems drawn again.
- **The units' own colours**: the original icons tell the sides apart by palette, tinting the whole icon blue, red or yellow. The 1.2 icons keep each unit's own colours; enemies and third parties get a 2-pixel ring in their side's colour, and your units none.
- **Acted units turn grey**: as in the original, a unit's icon turns grey once it has acted.
- **Matched to the original one for one**: each replaces one of the original icon textures, the game's logic is untouched, and F6 switches back to the original at any time. Six pairs of icons have identical pixels in the original (the white and the Titans Mk-II, for example); each pair shares one picture.

## Other changes

- **World map**: the original reuses two tiles of the story world map many times (a stretch of a northern edge and the Iranian plateau). They were left original before and showed as squares among the HD surface. They are now drawn as seamless HD tiles of their own, blended into the tiles around them.
- **Unit poses and portraits**: flaws in some of the pictures touched up.
- **GoShogun**: the Go Saber and Space Bazooka were white silhouettes in battle (#15). In the original, their colours are filled in during the battle by a palette animation; the HD pictures were exported with the static palette and came out white. They are now exported with the colours the battle shows. We checked every battle sprite, and only these two had the problem.

## The Switch's HD pack

The Switch has an HD pack of its own, `Marchwind64-HD-1.2-switch.zip`. Its pictures are the same as the desktop pack's, converted ahead of time into formats the Switch's GPU reads directly, which makes it larger; the desktop pack does not work on the Switch. Extract it to the SD card's root to get `/switch/Marchwind64/hd/`.

## Download and install

- Desktop and Android: download `Marchwind64-HD-1.2.zip` from the [install](/en/install/#hd) page and replace the old `hd` folder with it; on Android, import the zip in the app. An older HD pack still works, without the changes above, and the game's update check says there is a new one.
- Switch: download `Marchwind64-HD-1.2-switch.zip` and extract it to the SD card's root.
- Both are also on the GitHub [HD 1.2 release](https://github.com/dyzz/srw64-recomp/releases/tag/hd-1.2).

The new map icons and the touch-ups to the unit poses and portraits were drawn by OpenAI's image model (through Codex's image_gen) with the original graphics as reference, and are AI-generated. How the rest of the HD pack was made is in [Making the HD art](/en/blog/hd-art/).
