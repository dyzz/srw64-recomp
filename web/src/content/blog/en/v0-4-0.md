## Translation

- The Chinese and English translations have been polished throughout: every story and battle line has been rewritten to read naturally, and each name is now spelled one way everywhere.
- The changes to each line can be seen on the [Story](/en/story/) pages, where you can also suggest corrections.

## New

- **Android (experimental)**: for arm64 phones and handhelds with Android 9 or later and Vulkan 1.1. The HD pack can be imported from its zip, and the data folder opens in the Files app.
- **RetroArch filters and bezels**: on macOS (Metal), Linux and Steam Deck (Vulkan), Windows (D3D12 or Vulkan) and Android. Besides the built-in ones, you can use your own or RetroArch's filter and bezel folders.
- **Battle viewer**: on the title screen of the computer versions. Pick the attacker and defender, the weapon and the scene, and watch the battle animation.
- **Cheats**: on their own page in Options, and never saved.
- **Update check**: asks once at startup, checks the website for a newer version, and never downloads anything by itself.
- **Dialogue controls bar hides itself**: the button hints under the dialogue hide after 5 seconds and come back for 3 seconds on a direction key. They can be set to always show.

## Other changes

- The app is now called Marchwind64.
- The HD pack has versions of its own, starting with HD 1.0, and the game says when a newer one is out. Besides the user directory, the pack can also sit in the game's own folder.
- The series names flying past on the title screen and the unit names in the demo battle are shown in the reading language.
- ROM dumps in .v64 and .n64 format are accepted as well as .z64.
- An AI debug interface (MCP) for developers, off by default; turn it on under Options → About. See the [docs](/en/docs/).
- The project's own source code is now licensed GPL-3.0-or-later.

## Notes

- The macOS app is not notarized by Apple. The first time you open it, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.
- Z+START in battle is the original game's soft reset: it returns to the title screen and unsaved progress is lost.
