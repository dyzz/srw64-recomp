0.5.4.1 is a fix release for every platform. From 0.5.4, install over it: saves, settings and mods stay, and the HD pack stays 1.4. Upgrading from 0.5.3? See [the 0.5.4 notes](../v0-5-4/).

## Fixes

- **The HP gauge stays in its window.** With the battle animation off on a screen wider than 4:3, the gauge of the map's HP window sat to the right of the window, outside its frame. It had done so since 0.5.3, whichever renderer the game used. The same went for the windows of healing spirits such as Faith, Love and Miracle. The damage figure and "Miss" stay where they were, on the target.
- **Switch**: one cause of the occasional crash (error 2168-0006) in the first minute after starting is fixed: the game no longer hands out memory that hbmenu still holds.

## Mods

- **Recolour the original's weapon effects**: in the [unit maker](/en/mods/units/), Animation → Colours changes the palette entry by entry, and the original and HD images both follow.
- **Move a weapon's fire point**: drag the cross on the stage, and beams, shots and muzzle flashes move together.
- The maker can read your own ROM in the browser and draw the original effects on the stage to preview them. The ROM is only read in the browser and never uploaded.
- A package with changed colours needs 0.5.4.1 or later, and the export says so in it; 0.5.4 does not load it. A package's version requirement can now name a fix release such as 0.5.4.1. How to write it: [New units, characters and weapons](/en/docs/mod-units/).
