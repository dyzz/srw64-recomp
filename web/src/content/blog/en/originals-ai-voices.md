Super Robot Wars 64's original characters have never had voices in battle. This mod gives a voice to 843 battle lines of 12 of them: Ark, Selain, Brad, Manami, Ehrlich, Kurtz, Aisha and more.

- **Synthesized**: the voices are made with the speech synthesis model Irodori-TTS. They are not a real voice actor's voice.
- **Battles only**: the lines play during battle animations; story dialogue is unchanged.
- **Needs 0.5.2 or later**, about 22 MB.

## Download and install

Download the `.srw64mod` file from the [Mods page](/en/mods/#srw64.battle-voices-originals-ai) and drop it on the game window to install it. On Android, put it in `mods/` inside the Marchwind64 folder under Documents. The MOD manager disables or removes it at any time.
