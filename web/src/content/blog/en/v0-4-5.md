0.4.5 fixes a few problems players reported and adds some settings. The HD pack has not changed and is still HD 1.1; if you have it installed, there is nothing to download again.

## What's new

- **No more red and yellow patches in the battle sky.** A 0.4.4 problem on every platform: battle effects such as speed lines and flames were drawn as if they were the sky, leaving red and yellow patches across the skyline. Thanks to the player who sent a photo.
- **Ending a battle animation right as it starts no longer crashes.** Pressing the end key (X/B or R2) the moment an animation begins could crash; now it simply ends the animation.
- **Windows: the game starts when your user name or the game folder is in Chinese.** Before, it stopped with an error at once. This works on Windows 10 (version 1903 or later) and Windows 11.
- **New anti-aliasing switch** (Options → General). On by default; turning it off lightens the load on your graphics card, and may help if menus look garbled on some Intel graphics. Applies after restarting the game.
- **With battle animations off, a miss now shows MISS.** Before, an attack that missed showed nothing on the map. The map's battle results also play faster.
- **Choose the dialogue text size in the settings** (Options → UI), five sizes, remembered.
- **With HD on, the map unit icons can stay original** (Options → General), for those who prefer the original pixel sprites.
- **Android: the history button is back in dialogue.** 0.4.4 lost it when the touch buttons changed; it now sits where Back usually is, turns into Back while the history is open, and into Stop while skipping.
- **Windows: a crash leaves a diagnostic file**, included in the problem report from Options → Report. If the game crashes, please send me the report; it makes the cause much easier to find.

Please report problems on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dyzz/srw64-recomp/issues); in the game, Options → Report copies the platform info and exports a problem report.

## Android chips tested

The game starts and plays on real phones with these chips (Android 12–16). Entry-level chips like the Dimensity 6100+ run at about 14 FPS; the others mostly at 30. A chip that is not listed has simply not been tested yet; reports on Issues are welcome.

- Qualcomm: Snapdragon 865/870 (Adreno 650; Galaxy Tab S7, POCO F3), 888 (Adreno 660; Galaxy S21 Ultra), 8 Gen 1 (Adreno 730; Galaxy S22, Xiaomi 12), 8 Gen 2 (Adreno 740; Xiaomi 13, Galaxy S23), 8 Gen 3 (Adreno 750; Galaxy S24 Ultra), 8 Elite (Adreno 830; Galaxy S25)
- MediaTek: Dimensity 7300 (Mali-G615; Solana Seeker), Dimensity 6100+ (Mali-G57; Galaxy A15)
- Samsung: Exynos 1380 (Mali-G68; Galaxy A35)
- Google: Tensor G2 (Mali-G710; Pixel 7), Tensor G3 (Mali-G715; Pixel 8)

If you play on a Dimensity 8200 phone, please let us know whether it runs well.
