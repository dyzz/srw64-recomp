0.4.9 brings more Snapdragon phones in, fixes a few things on the battle page and adds some conveniences. The HD pack has not changed and is still HD 1.1.

## Fixes

- **Android: Snapdragon 855/860, 778G, 765G and 730/732G phones no longer close at start** (Poco X3 Pro, Pixel 4/4a/5, Galaxy A73 and others). Thanks to the Poco X3 Pro player who tested it. Older Snapdragon phones still on Android 9 or 10 now ask you to update Android instead of closing.
- A crash when casting 友情 or similar spirits on the battle page during the enemy's turn.
- A flash when choosing Evade or Defend on the battle page.
- An extra confirm sound in dialogue with nothing pressed.
- Misaligned weapon ranges and negative hit modifiers in the original-style screens.

## New features

- **Unified weapon upgrades** (Options → UI → Weapon upgrades; Unified by default): all of a unit's weapons, every form included, go up together to one level, for the same total price as one by one; weapons that join later catch up for free. The full-upgrade bonuses show one after another, and levels past the original cap show as yellow triangles. Choose Per weapon (original) for the original's way.
- **Load the suspend save from the map**: the Load button beside Info on Android, a button on the stage info page, or F9 on the keyboard. It asks first, showing the save's turn and funds, and works in the middle of a battle too.
- **Carry finished hidden elements into a new playthrough** (a new difficulty rule, off by default): a hidden element finished in an earlier playthrough joins right after the stage where it would have joined.
- **A new cheat: unlimited actions** for your units.
- **Pilot pages show the spirits still to learn**, with their levels. An enemy's pilot page shows its learned spirits and SP, and every enemy's numbers can be seen from the start of the stage.
- **The Link page is a list of works**, with the chosen one's units and pilots in a panel beside it.
- **Smaller downloads**: the desktop packages are about 12 MB smaller, and the Android APK went from 88 MB to 34 MB.

For other problems, Options → Report in the game copies the platform info and exports a problem report. Which Android chips have been tested is on the [Android compatibility list](/en/android/).
