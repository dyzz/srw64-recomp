SRW64 Symbols (content/fonts/SRW64Symbols.ttf)
===============================================

This font holds the four symbols the SRW64 text needs that HarmonyOS Sans lacks:
U+25B6 ▶, U+25B7 ▷, U+25C0 ◀ and U+1F527 🔧. It is built by
tools/content/build_symbol_font.py.

- The three triangles are outlines taken from DejaVu Sans (Book), rescaled and
  repositioned. DejaVu Sans is covered by the licence reproduced below: Bitstream
  Vera copyright and licence, DejaVu changes in the public domain. As that licence
  requires for modified fonts, this font's name contains neither "Bitstream" nor
  "Vera", and the notices below travel with every copy.
- The wrench glyph was drawn for the SRW64 project after the original game's repair
  icon and is distributed under the same terms as the rest of this font.
