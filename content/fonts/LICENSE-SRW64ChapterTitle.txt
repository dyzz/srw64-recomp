SRW64 Chapter Title (content/fonts/SRW64ChapterTitle.ttf)
=========================================================

The brush lettering of the Super Robot Taisen 64 chapter title cards as a font,
for the Japanese title cards the host draws natively. Every glyph is traced from
the cards' own images in the game ROM: tools/content/build_title_fonts.py cuts
the cards into characters by their transcription and traces each with potrace.
It holds only the 344 characters the cards use.

The lettering is part of Super Robot Taisen 64 (c) BANPRESTO 1999; it is not
covered by this project's GPL licence.
