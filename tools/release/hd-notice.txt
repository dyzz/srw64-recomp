Marchwind64 — HD 图片包 / HD image pack

安装：把这个 hd 文件夹放进用户目录，
  macOS：~/Library/Application Support/SRW64Recomp/hd
打开游戏即为 HD。F6 或设置窗口的“图片”可以随时切回原版。
这个包只能配同一版本的应用使用；换版本时整个替换。

来源说明
- 头像、场间背景、宇宙物件和标题 Logo 与火焰，
  由阿里云百炼的通义千问图像模型（qwen-image-3.0 / qwen-image-3.0-pro）
  以原版画面为参考生成；场间背景扩展到 16:9 时两侧由通义万相（wanx2.1-imageedit）补画。
  属于 AI 生成内容。
- 剧情世界地图和战术地图的底图，以及部分战斗 cut-in 与战斗中的机体额外图，
  由 OpenAI 的图像模型（通过 Codex 的 image_gen）以原版画面为参考绘制，属于 AI 生成内容。
- 机体立绘、地图上的机体图标、战斗 cut-in 与道具、战斗地面贴图，
  用本地放大模型（4x-UltraSharpV2、4x-PixelPerfectV4）从原版像素图放大重绘；
  其余机体额外图由 HD 立绘推导；战术地图的色号图（水面等颜色循环用）由原版地图像素放大而来。
  这几类图由原版画面衍生。
- 对话框和战斗界面边框由代码绘制；世界地图的舰船、地标与地点标记是自制模型。
- BANPRESTO 标志、GAME OVER 和窗口边框由游戏运行时从原版画面算出，不在包里。

Install: put this hd folder in the user directory
  (macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/SRW64Recomp/hd). The game then starts in HD;
F6 or Images in the settings window switches back to the original. Use the pack with
the app of the same version, and replace it as a whole when you update.

The portraits, intermission backgrounds, space objects and the title logo and flames
are AI-generated with Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen image models qwen-image-3.0 /
qwen-image-3.0-pro; the sides of the intermission backgrounds widened to 16:9 with
Wanx wanx2.1-imageedit), and the story world map, the tactical map bases and some
battle cut-ins and extra battle unit images with OpenAI's image model (Codex
image_gen), using the original graphics as reference. The unit poses, the map unit
icons, the battle cut-ins and props and the battle ground textures are upscaled and
redrawn from the original pixel art with local upscaling models (4x-UltraSharpV2,
4x-PixelPerfectV4), the other extra battle unit images are derived from the HD unit
poses, and the tactical maps' palette-index maps (for water and other colour cycling)
are upscaled from the original map pixels: these images are derived from the original
graphics. The dialogue and battle frames are drawn by code; the world-map ships,
landmarks and the location marker are models made for this project. The BANPRESTO logo, GAME OVER and the window frames are
computed from the original graphics by the game at run time and are not in the pack.
